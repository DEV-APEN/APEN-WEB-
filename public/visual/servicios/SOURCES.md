# Service header artwork

These four illustrative sector images were generated with OpenAI image generation
for APEN service-detail headers. They do not depict verified APEN facilities,
projects, offices or clients. No institutional logos are part of the artwork.

- `hidrocarburos-header.webp`: petroleum refinery and storage infrastructure.
- `energia-header.webp`: electrical substation and transmission infrastructure.
- `legal-header.webp`: scales of justice and case files in a law office.
- `financiamiento-header.webp`: infrastructure model and financial planning.

Optimized from the generated PNG originals to WebP, quality 82, 1536 x 1024.
One shared image per sector is reused across its service pages.
